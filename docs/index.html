Rotato

Discover, collect, and rotate wallpapers on Android, built for foldables like the Pixel Fold.

Download APK

Features

Discover

Swipe a full-screen feed from your sources. A floating dock holds Save, Set, Library, Share and Skip, and pulls up for every detail and tag.

For you

Discover learns from what you save, set, and skip, and ranks the feed toward your taste. Turn it off or reset it any time.

Made for foldables

Pixel Fold aware: fold-friendly tags, smart per-screen crops, cover and inner fold pairs, and a collections rail when unfolded.

Collections

Save to several collections at once, reorder and merge them, build smart collections from tag rules, and lock private ones behind biometrics.

Auto rotation

Wallpapers change on your schedule, from every 15 minutes to once a day, with per-screen pools for home and lock.

Schedules

Switch collections automatically on specific days and times.

Content filter

One switch in Settings hides every NSFW feature, image, and locked collection until you turn it back on.

Plugin store

Install source plugins from the official store, add your own index, and restore built-in sources if you remove them.

MAL integration

Filter wallpapers by your MyAnimeList list, so you only see art from shows you love.

Backups

Export everything to a file, or let Rotato save a backup there daily.

Privacy first

No Rotato account, no analytics, no ads. Your collections and settings stay on your device.
